# M3 Subscription and perks audit

Compare what the student pays for against the perks catalog below and against what their own school already gives them. Produce a short, ranked list of "switch or cancel" moves with yearly savings. Cubbie never cancels anything: it gives the link and the steps.

## Steps

1. Collect the student's recurring payments. Start from memory (Stretch subscriptions and Stretch memberships). If Gmail is connected, search it read-only for receipts, "your subscription", "free trial" and "will renew" emails. If Gmail is not connected, ask once to connect it. If the student would rather not, ask them to list what they pay, or to text receipt or subscription-confirmation screenshots. Do not ask for bank statements or logins.
2. Look for five kinds of findings:
   - Duplicates: two AI chatbots, two music services, overlapping streaming.
   - Student pricing missed: a student plan exists for something they pay full price for.
   - Already free from school: software, gym, AI tools, news, transit, tutoring, events.
   - Free trials about to convert to a paid plan (from receipt emails when Gmail is linked).
   - Big recurring costs with a cheaper alternative: a parking permit versus a transit pass, a gym versus the school's rec center.
3. Check the school's own pages. Search for the student's university with "student software", "student discounts", "transit pass", "recreation center" and "student health services", read the pages you find, and use only what they say. For ASU, start from the catalog below, then confirm on the page when you can.
4. Do the math. Yearly savings are (current monthly cost minus new monthly cost) times 12. If you do not know what the student pays, ask or use the "regular price" in the catalog and say "about". For a promotion that ends, show the first year and what it becomes after.
5. Send one message with the top three to five moves, biggest yearly savings first. Each line: what to switch or cancel, the yearly saving, the link on its own line, and the step. End with "I can't cancel things for you, but I can walk you through any of these."

Example reply:

```text
3 ways to keep money:
1. Use ASU's free gym instead of paying for one: about $0 vs what you pay now. Ask if you want me to look up your gym's cancel steps.
2. Spotify student plan: $6.99/mo vs $12.99, saves about $72/yr.
https://www.spotify.com/us/student/
3. You and your school both give you an AI chatbot. ASU's is free:
https://ai.asu.edu/ai-tools/chatgpt-edu
I can't cancel things for you, but I can walk you through any of these.
```

## Catalog

Everything below was checked against the official page on 2026-10-24. Prices and offers change. Say "about" and "as of late October 2026" when you quote a number, and check the linked page again before relying on a number that is more than a few weeks old.

### Delivery and shopping memberships

- DoorDash DashPass. Regular price is $9.99 a month plus tax. DoorDash also offers a Free Student DashPass: $0 a month for 12 months, then $4.99 a month, for eligible college students at select universities, verified through SheerID. Be careful: DoorDash's terms page says it applies to sign-ups after 8/1/2026, its FAQ says the sign-up window ran Aug 17 to Oct 12, 2026, and neither page lists ASU for the new offer. Never tell a student it is free or that they qualify. Tell them to check whether they are eligible, and send them to doordash.com/dashpass/student. If they pay full price and are a student, "you might qualify for a student plan" is the most you can say.
  Sources: https://help.doordash.com/en-us/consumers/article/free-student-dashpass-terms-and-conditions and https://help.doordash.com/en-us/consumers/article/free-student-dashpass-faqs
- Uber One for students (US). $4.99 a month after a 4-week free trial, with 18+ student verification. Benefits listed include $0 delivery fees on eligible orders above the minimum and member-only student deals. Source: https://www.uber.com/uber-one/student
- Amazon Prime Student. Not verified in this build because Amazon's page blocks automated reading. Search for the current student price and trial length and use only what you read. Do not quote a number from memory.

### Music and streaming (US)

- Spotify Premium Student: $6.99 a month after a 1-month free trial, includes Hulu with ads. Regular Premium is $12.99 a month. Verified through SheerID, up to 12 months at a time, re-verified each year. Source: https://www.spotify.com/us/student/
- Apple Music Student: $6.99 a month with the first month free, includes Apple TV; the Individual plan is $11.99 a month. Student pricing for up to four years while the student stays enrolled. Source: https://www.apple.com/apple-music/

### Arizona State University (check each with the student)

- Gym: the Sun Devil Fitness Complexes are free on all four campuses with an ASU ID, with free group wellness classes. A paid gym membership is a likely cancel. Source: https://heysunny.asu.edu/blog/free-tuition
- Software: free or discounted programs such as Microsoft Office and engineering and STEM tools through the My Apps portal on My ASU. Adobe Creative Cloud is described by ASU Enterprise Technology as free for all students, but the page gives no sign-up steps, so send the student to My Apps or the ASU Digital Backpack and tell them to confirm. Sources: https://heysunny.asu.edu/blog/free-tuition and https://tech.asu.edu/briefs/add-adobe-creative-cloud
- AI tools: ChatGPT Edu is free to ASU students. Sign up through the Self-Subscription Portal ("ChatGPT: User Account"), then sign in with ASURITE and DUO. Student accounts have no credit allocation and cannot use credit-consuming features, so a paid chatbot plan may still add something. Be fair about that. Source: https://ai.asu.edu/ai-tools/chatgpt-edu
- News: digital New York Times and Wall Street Journal subscriptions for enrolled students (instructions linked from the heysunny page above).
- Transit: the U-Pass is $150 for the academic year (Aug 16 to May 15, and $75 if bought after Jan 1) with unlimited rides on Valley Metro light rail and local, express and RAPID buses; ASU subsidizes it. Compare against a parking permit: ask what the student pays, or search the ASU Parking and Transit Services site for the current permit price. Do not state a parking price from memory. Source: https://cfo.asu.edu/transit-passes
- Events and extras: tickets to ASU athletic events are included in tuition and fees through the Sun Devil Gameday app, and the ASU Library Makerspace is free to students. Source: https://heysunny.asu.edu/blog/free-tuition
- Health clinic: ASU Health Services exists, but its billing page lists no flat prices and no health fee that makes visits free. Visits are billed to insurance or the student account. Do not tell a student visits are free; suggest they call the billing office (480-965-8559) to ask what a visit costs. Source: https://eoss.asu.edu/health/billing-insurance

### Other schools

Search for the school's student software, recreation center, transit, health services and discounts pages, read them, and use only what they say. Say you checked the school's site and that details may differ at the counter.
